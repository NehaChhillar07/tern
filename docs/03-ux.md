Version 1 of this document described a sidebar app with separate pages. It was replaced: Tern is an agent, so the interface is one thread with no navigation.

---

## 1. Structure

```
Thread (the only screen)
├─ Headline               Tern's read of where you stand
├─ While you were away    what Tern did by itself, and what needs you
├─ Your applications      boarding passes, scroll sideways
├─ Needs you this week    to-do rows
├─ Conversation           your messages, Tern's runs and results
└─ Composer               pinned to the bottom, with up to three suggestions

Sheets (slide over the thread)
├─ Boarding pass          one application: status stamp, apply button, what's left, facts
├─ Passport               what Tern knows about you, and your documents
├─ Draft                  a statement, reference request or CV being written
├─ Compare                up to four courses side by side
└─ Settings               reminders, export or delete my data
```

There is no shortlist page and no tracker page. The shortlist is something Tern shows in the thread when asked; the tracker is the row of boarding passes.

How people reach things without navigation:
- **Tern brings it up** in "While you were away" when it matters.
- **Suggestion pills** above the composer offer the three most likely next moves.
- **Asking** always works: "show my shortlist", "what's due", "open Harrowgate".
- **Passport** is the one fixed button, top right.

---

## 2. Key flows

| Flow | What happens |
|---|---|
| **First visit** | Headline: "Hi. Drop your CV and I'll tell you where you stand." The composer has an attach button. No sign-up wall before the first answer. |
| **Assessment** | A run ("Reading your CV"), then two short lists: what is working for you and what is worth fixing. Passport fills in. |
| **Preferences** | Tern asks one or two questions at a time. Each answer shows up in Passport. Skipped questions get a stated default. |
| **Finding courses** | A run shows the steps (profile, countries searched, pages opened, facts checked). Result rows follow, each with fit, deadline, fee and one reason. |
| **Tracking** | "Track" turns a result into a boarding pass. Toast: "Boarding pass added". |
| **Coming back** | Headline counts down to the nearest deadline. "While you were away" lists changes Tern caught and drafts waiting for review. |
| **Doing a task** | A to-do or a "?" row opens the draft in the thread or a sheet. Tern never sends, submits or pays; the screen says who does. |
| **Applying** | "Apply on the university site" opens the official page in a new tab. The user marks it submitted; the stamp changes. |
| **Offer and visa** | The pass gains the offer conditions, then the country's official visa steps with sources. |

## 3. States

| Surface | Empty | Working | Went wrong |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Thread, first visit** | Headline invites a CV upload. No passes, no to-dos, no empty boxes. | n/a | n/a |
| **While you were away** | "Nothing changed since yesterday. I checked 14 pages." | n/a | "I couldn't reach 2 university sites. I'll try again tonight." |
| **Boarding passes** | Section is hidden until the first course is tracked. | A new pass rises into the row. | n/a |
| **Run** | n/a | Yellow dot pulses; steps tick in; long runs keep going if the user leaves and report back in "While you were away". | The failed step is named: "Couldn't open Wexcombe's fees page. The other 41 are checked." |
| **Facts** | n/a | "Checking again…" replaces the date. | Muted, no underline: "Not checked yet. See the official page." |
| **Composer** | Placeholder: "Ask Tern, or tell it what to do" | Send is disabled while a run is in progress. | "That didn't send. Check your connection and try again." The text stays in the field. |

Deadlines: within 30 days the date turns red and the headline counts it. A deadline that moved is red on the pass and called out in "While you were away". After a deadline passes the pass reads "Gate closed".

## 4. What is in the preview, and what is not

**In the preview:** returning-user home, one agent run with results and tracking, the boarding pass sheet with checklist and sourced facts, the Passport sheet, the reference-request draft, light and dark themes, phone and desktop.

**Still to design:** first visit and CV upload, the preference questions, the statement-of-purpose writing sheet, Compare, offer and visa steps on the pass, Settings, sign-in, reminder emails.

**Reviews:** the preview was checked by screenshot at 1280px and 390px in both themes. The formal design critique, accessibility review and interface-guidelines check have not been run; they run on each real screen as it is built.
